## Pattern Notes

- Subject line: "Dust and Miscellaneous Portal Submission Confirmation"
- Sender: [AQDIMPACT@maricopa.gov](mailto:AQDIMPACT@maricopa.gov) (different from [no-reply@maricopa.gov](mailto:no-reply@maricopa.gov))
- Sent immediately after online portal submission
- No Application ID yet (assigned after processing)
- No Facility ID (assigned after approval)
- Payment reminder included

## Workflow Position

This is the FIRST notification in the permit lifecycle:
1. **Submission Confirmation** (this) - Application received
2. Payment Confirmation - Fee paid via PointAndPay
3. Permit Issued OR Rejected - County decision

## Frequency

High volume - one per application submitted through portal
