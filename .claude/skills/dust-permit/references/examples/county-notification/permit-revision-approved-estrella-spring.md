## Pattern Notes

- Subject line format: "[Contractor] - [Project Name] Revision"
- Subject includes "Revision" to distinguish from new permit issuance

## Revision vs New Permit

- **New Permit:** Subject is "Dust Permit Issued"
- **Revision:** Subject includes contractor name and "Revision"
- Body format is identical for both

## When Revisions Occur

- Acreage changes (increase or decrease)
- Site boundary modifications
- BMP changes
- Contact information updates
- Project timeline extensions
