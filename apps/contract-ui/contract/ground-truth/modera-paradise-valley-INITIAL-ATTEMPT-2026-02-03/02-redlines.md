## Quick Reference - What We Include vs. Don't Include

### INCLUDED IN SCOPE

- SWPPP Narrative design
- Initial BMP installation (filter sock, rock entrances, curb inlet protection, signs, spill kits)
- 60 inspections (biweekly + rain events)
- Dust Control Permit + 2 renewals
- Backflow certification and rental
- Rumble grate rental
- 1 mobilization

### NOT INCLUDED IN SCOPE

- BMP maintenance
- BMP repair
- BMP removal
- Additional mobilizations (beyond initial)
- NOI/NOT filing
- Record retention
- Sediment removal
- Sign replacement
- Consulting services

### AVAILABLE ON T&M (Section K rates)

- All maintenance
- All repairs
- BMP removal
- Additional mobilizations
- Additional inspections beyond 60
- Sediment removal
- Material replacements
