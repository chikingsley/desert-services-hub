### Scope Clarifications

1. **Exhibit A-1 Scope of Work - Maintenance Language**

   Our estimate includes installation and inspections only. The contract includes maintenance, repair, and removal language throughout Exhibit A-1 that is outside our quoted scope. We recommend the following:

   - Reference **Section K (Additives/Alternates)** for all maintenance and additional work. Our rate sheet covers these items on a T&M basis.
   - Remove or strike maintenance language from:
     - EC-5 Stabilized Construction Entrance ("maintain and remove")
     - SPC-5 Filter Sock ("maintain and remove")  
     - SPC-07 Curb Inlet Protection ("maintain and remove")
     - Project Specific Items - Maintenance subsection (items C, D, E)
     - General SWPPP Items sections G, H, I, J, Q

2. **Section D1 - Permits**

   Current language states "all costs of all permits, fees, and licenses." Our scope includes only the permits specified in our estimate:
   - Dust Control Permit (1)
   - Dust Permit Renewals (2)

   Recommend language: "Subcontractor shall include only the costs of permits and fees included in the estimate. No additional permits or fees are included unless specified in the final Schedule of Values."

3. **Section F - Hoisting/Scaffolding/Stocking**

   This section is not applicable to SWPPP scope. Recommend removing or marking as N/A.

4. **Inspections Quantity**

   Please confirm 60 inspections are included per the Schedule of Values. Contract language should specify this quantity.

5. **Drop Inlet Protection**

   Contract excludes drop inlet protection ("to be provided by storm sewer Contractor"). Please confirm this is correct and Desert Services is NOT responsible for drop inlets.
