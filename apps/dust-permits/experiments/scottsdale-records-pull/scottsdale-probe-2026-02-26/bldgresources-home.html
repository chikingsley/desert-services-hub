Starting January 6, 2026, the City will officially launch SPUR, our brand-new permitting system designed to make your experience faster, easier, and more efficient.

If your project began before this date, don’t worry — it will continue in our current system or, when possible, be transitioned smoothly into SPUR. Moving forward, all new applications will be submitted directly through SPUR.

We truly appreciate your patience and understanding as we make this important upgrade. We're confident that SPUR will bring big improvements for everyone, and we’re here to support you every step of the way!
