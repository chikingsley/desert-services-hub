Document Search

Search documents in our public records database for Planning and Development. A property address search will deliver the best results.

  • Enter any part of an address to begin the search.
  • Click a document link to download to your desktop or mobile device.
  • Click on Sort to list documents by Full Address, Case, Permit or Plan Number, Document Name, Description by the Sort button.

Street Number

Enter the numeric identifier for a land parcel, house, building or other feature, as defined by the official address authority for the given jurisdiction.

Example: 1234 Main Street, 100

Street Name

Enter the official name of a street as assigned by a local governing authority, or an alternate (alias) name that is used and recognized, excluding street types, directionals, and modifiers..

Example: 1234 Main Street, 100

Street Post Type

Enter the element of the complete street name following the street name element that indicates the type of street..

Example: 1234 Main ST, 100

Planning and Development Case Number

Planning and Development Plan Number

Planning and Development Building Permit Number
