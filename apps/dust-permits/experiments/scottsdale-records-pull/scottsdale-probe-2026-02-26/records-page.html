Records

In this section
  1. City of Scottsdale
  2. Planning and Development
  3. Records

Planning and Development Services Records encourage customers to take advantage of our online services.

Property Information

This online request allows users to enter an address or parcel number to:

  • Find zoning and district standards information related to a property
  • View history of permits issued, plan reviews, cases and property records
  • Request setbacks, allowed land uses, sign information and flood hazard determination
  • View interactive map to identify parcel boundaries, easements and a selection of other data

View & Request Property Information

Site Plans (a detailed diagram showing a property's layout in relation to its surroundings) can be found at Document Search. Building plans are destroyed 180 days after completion or expiration. Plans are the intellectual property of either the architect or engineer. Please contact the architect or engineer to obtain copies, this may be listed on the original building permit.

For current Flood Insurance Rate Map (FIRM) submit a Flood Hazard request on Property Information.

Lookup Tools

Document Search

Search our records repository for permits, certificate of occupancy, site plans, and elevation certificates plus more.
GIS Maps & Data

Various maps, which allows you to find Building Activity, Code Enforcement, Certificate of Occupancy, Green Building Permits, Quarter Section Maps, Aerials, etc.
Open Data Portal

Use the city’s Open Data Portal to access information on building permits, certificates of occupancy, and more.
As-Builts

Submit a public records request to obtain As-Built plans, which contain detailed information about existing city infrastructure.
Inspection Services Information

Check the status of Permits, Schedule Inspections, and find contact information for Inspectors.
Right-of-Way Permits

Lookup Right-of-Way permit details and schedule inspections.
Case Files Status Lookup

Search for individual or multiple case file information.

Submit a Public Record Request

If you are unable to find the records using the options above you may submit a public records request. See the fee schedule for any costs associated with this service.
