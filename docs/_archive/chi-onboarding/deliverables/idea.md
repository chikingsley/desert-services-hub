[Contract Process - Estimating] The following items should lie with estimating department for the reasons given: 

Reconciling the estimate against the customer’s requested SOV or work authorization AND working with the customer on any SOV discrepancies until that piece is finalized
Reason 1: Context. Estimator has context on what was priced and why. 
Reason 2: Continuity. The customer likely already spoke with the estimator about pricing. It makes sense for that conversation to continue there before coming to contracts.
Reason 3: Role Scope. Pricing and negotiations regarding pricing are business decisions made higher up than me. Sales and estimating frequently make pricing updates that don’t match the official pricing that I have. That’s fine in general but it means updating/reconciling that pricing should lie directly with who’s making the decisions. 
Marking job as won in Monday, Updating the Award Total, Updating Awarded Dates, Creating the new job in QuickBooks, Moving the Estimate to that new job in QuickBooks 
Reason 1: Context. They will know the project name, contractor, estimate a lot quicker since they created it and thus finding projects is quicker
Reason 2: Role Scope. Won estimates, finalized numbers are a part of the estimating departments metrics. They should own them.
Reason 3: Continuity. If they create/reconcile/negotiate estimate/SOV, it makes sense for them to directly update the estimate, mark the win, and complete the loop they started in QuickBooks.
Ordering SWPPP Plan (was already owned by estimating but should stay)
Reason: Role Scope. They need the SWPPP plan to complete the final estimate on the project. They should be responsible for ordering and delivering that completed scope item to the customer along with the updated estimate. 

[Project Coordinator Role] Coordinators should own the following:
Follow up with customer after pre-contract work is completed to get contract/work order/invoice information. 
Contract review regarding contract language, expectation discrepancies, and the related communication about those issues.
Project initiation/clarification through gathering project information from the contract and getting project requirements if we didn’t start work yet
I changed this from initiation because most of the time, the activities are being done AFTER we have done work or concurrent with work being done. As a result, often onsite requirements, safety, etc is being handled by the SSM or SWPPP Service Director before I ever hear about the project.
Sending SDS, Safety Documentation, Contract requirements.
