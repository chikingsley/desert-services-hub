**Source:** Arizona Department of Environmental Quality (ADEQ)

**Address:** 1110 West Washington, Phoenix, AZ 85007

**Website:** www.azdeq.gov

---

## Overview

This General Permit allows for discharges of wastewater generated from washing vehicles and equipment.

### NOT Authorized Under This Permit

The following discharges are **NOT** allowed:
- Sanitary sewage
- Vehicle lubricating oils
- Antifreeze
- Gasoline
- Paints
- Varnishes
- Solvents
- Pesticides or fertilizers
- Discharges from washing the interior of vessels used to transport fuel products or chemicals
- Washing of equipment contaminated with fuel products or chemicals

**If the proposed discharge, design or operations do not conform to the rule, the owner or operator must obtain an individual APP.**

  - Include design drawings, specifications and calculations per A.A.C. R18-9-D303(C)(1)

- [ ] Description of wash operations

- [ ] Description of equipment, detergents or additives used

- [ ] Location, dimensions, and construction of all impoundments, subsurface disposal systems, or other disposal facilities

- [ ] Demonstration that the facility is adequately sized
  - Must handle projected maximum flows plus any stormwater run-on

## Key Thresholds and Requirements

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Oil/Water Separator Treatment** | 15 ppm or less |
| **Max Daily Flow (Unlined/Subsurface)** | Less than 3,000 gallons/day |
| **Water Supply Well Setback** | Minimum 100' horizontal distance |
| **Groundwater Depth** | Bottom must be 50'+ above static water level |
| **Engine Wash Discharge** | Must use lined surface impoundment |
| **Mining Wash Discharge** | Must use lined surface impoundment |
