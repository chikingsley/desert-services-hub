# User Roles & Responsibilities in myDEQ

**ADEQ Publication No.:** TM-18-28

**Source:** Arizona Department of Environmental Quality (ADEQ)

## Overview

The myDEQ system uses a hierarchical role-based access model with multiple user types, each with specific responsibilities and authority levels.

## Responsible Corporate Officer (RCO)

**Main account holder, may manage one or more organizations:**

- Has the ability to legally bind the organization
- Must certify the Subscriber/Signature Agreement
- Submits the necessary documents for initial account setup
- Delegates authority in myDEQ by adding DROs to their account
- Can manage multiple organizations/entities

### Organization

This may be an actual organization like a business or government agency, or another type of entity for certain services. For example:
- If a private citizen needs a drywell on their property registered with ADEQ, the citizen would act as RCO
- The citizen would assign their property as the Organization
- They would have legal authority to bind the property

## Delegated Responsible Officer (DRO)

**Each organization may have one or more DROs to act on the RCO's behalf in myDEQ.**

**Requirements:**
- Adopting a DRO requires a signed and notarized Signature Agreement
- The agreement is emailed when the DRO is added to the account

**Responsibilities:**
- Possesses authority delegated by the RCO to act on their behalf
- Oversees all myDEQ account activities for an organization
- Can authorize Submitters and Data Entry users

## Submitter

**Adopting submitters requires signed and notarized Signature Agreements** (emailed when each Submitter is added to the account).

**Responsibilities:**
- Submit compliance reports
- Modify some permit information for any permit or registration the RCO or DRO chooses to allow
- Cannot make certain administrative changes

## Data Entry

**No notarized agreement required**

**Responsibilities:**
- Can be assigned to prepare compliance report data for the other users to submit
- Cannot directly submit data to ADEQ
- Useful for data preparation and organization

## Role Hierarchy and Delegation

```text
RCO (Main Authority)
  ├── DRO (Multiple possible)
  │   ├── Submitter (Multiple possible)
  │   └── Data Entry (Multiple possible)
  └── Organization Management
```

- RCOs delegate to DROs
- DROs can supervise Submitters and Data Entry users
- Data Entry users support Submitters but cannot submit directly
- Each role has specific limitations on what can be modified or submitted

## Key Distinctions

| Role | Can Submit? | Requires Notarization? | Can Authorize Others? |
|------|-------------|----------------------|----------------------|
| RCO | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| DRO | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Submitter | Yes | Yes | No |
| Data Entry | No | No | No |

## Important Notes

- The RCO has ultimate legal authority to bind the organization
- All roles except Data Entry require notarized Signature Agreements
- Multiple DROs, Submitters, and Data Entry users can be assigned to a single organization
- Each role should be assigned based on actual job responsibilities
- DROs act with the authority delegated by the RCO
