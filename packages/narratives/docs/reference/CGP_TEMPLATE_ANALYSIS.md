# CGP Template Full Analysis

## Template Overview

**Document:** cgp_p3_template.docx
**Type:** Arizona Construction General Permit (AZG2025-001)
**Size:** 2,391 lines
**Purpose:** SWPPP for construction sites (NOT industrial facilities)

## Template Structure

### 13 Major Sections

1. **Contact Information/Responsible Parties**
2. **Site Evaluation, Assessment, and Planning**
3. **Erosion and Sediment Controls**
4. **Pollution Prevention Requirements**
5. **Inspections**
6. **Corrective Action**
7. **Delegation of Authority**
8. **Training**
9. **SWPPP Updates and Modifications**
10. **Posting SWPPP, SWPPP Review, and Making SWPPPs Available**
11. **Stormwater Monitoring**
12. **Reporting Requirements**
13. **Notice of Termination (NOT)**

## Extraction Difficulty Assessment

| Data Type | Difficulty | Reason |
|-----------|----------|--------|
| **Contact info from Estimate** | 🟢 Easy | Structured, consistent location (page 1) |
| **Project address from Storm Plan** | 🟢 Easy | Cover sheet, consistent format |
| **Lat/Long from Storm Plan** | 🟡 Medium | Cover sheet, but needs format conversion |
| **Site area from Storm Plan** | 🟢 Easy | LOT DATA section |
| **BMP list from Estimate** | 🟡 Medium | Table format, need to parse line items |
| **Receiving waters from Storm Plan** | 🟢 Easy | Cover sheet or notes section |
| **Project dates from Estimate** | 🟡 Medium | May be in estimate or inferred from inspection schedule |
| **% Impervious calculations** | 🔴 Hard | May not be in any document - needs calculation |
| **Environmental review info** | 🔴 Hard | Separate document, complex checkboxes |
