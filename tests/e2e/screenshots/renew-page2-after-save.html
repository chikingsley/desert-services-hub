Welcome to the Maricopa County Air Quality Department's Dust and Miscellaneous Portal for permit applications, registrations, notifications and invoice payments.

To complete tasks within the portal, you must first register as a new user or login to your existing account.  To pay an invoice, you do not need to register or login. Select 'Invoices' from the upper right hand corner, select 'Pay Invoice' from the upper left, and enter your Invoice ID exactly as shown (e.g., IV012345). A public record search for existing application information does not require a user registration or login.

For best performance, use Google Chrome as your internet browser, and enable pop-ups from Maricopa.gov. For additional assistance, select the Help tab located in the upper right-hand corner of the screen.
